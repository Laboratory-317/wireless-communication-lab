# About the laboratory

We study 5G/6G wireless systems, error-correcting codes, multiple access and intelligent surfaces.

The laboratory is based at the Department of Telecommunications and Basic Principles of Radio Engineering, TUSUR, in Tomsk.
- [Contact](mailto:laboratory317@gmail.com)

# Research Areas

## 5G/6G

![Antenna array and directional signal transmission](assets/research/wireless-en.svg)

Multi-antenna systems, signal processing and radio channel design for mobile communications. We study ways to improve data transmission and radio resource efficiency. [Laboratory projects](projects.html).

## Polar codes

![Polar code transform for four input bits](assets/research/polar-en.svg)

Error-correcting codes for reliable information transmission. Research includes adapting polar codes to channel conditions and using them in multiple access systems. [Research publications](publications.html).

## Multiple access

![Conceptual allocation of time-frequency resources to different users](assets/research/access-en.svg)

Sharing radio resources among multiple users. The laboratory studies NOMA, SCMA and RSMA, resource allocation and comparisons of access techniques. [Research results](publications.html).

## Reconfigurable metasurfaces

![A reconfigurable metasurface directs a radio signal toward a 6G device](assets/research/reconfigurable-metasurface.png)

Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, including RIS and STAR-RIS, offer control over radio wave propagation. We model radio channels and develop reflecting surfaces. [Related projects](projects.html).
