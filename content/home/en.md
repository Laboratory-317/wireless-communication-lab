# About the laboratory

Wireless communications — from coding methods to next-generation systems. The laboratory brings together research in 5G/6G, polar codes, multiple access and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces.

The laboratory is based at the Department of Telecommunications and Basic Principles of Radio Engineering, TUSUR, in Tomsk.
- [Contact](mailto:laboratory317@gmail.com)

# Research Areas

## 5G/6G

![Antenna array and directional signal transmission](assets/research/wireless.svg)

Multi-antenna systems, signal processing and radio channel design for mobile communications. The focus is on efficient data transmission and use of network resources. [Laboratory projects](projects.html).

## Polar codes

![Polar code transform for four input bits](assets/research/polar.svg)

Error-correcting codes for reliable information transmission. Research includes adapting polar codes to channel conditions and using them in multiple access systems. [Research publications](publications.html).

## Multiple access

![Conceptual allocation of time-frequency resources to different users](assets/research/access.svg)

Sharing radio resources among multiple users. The laboratory studies NOMA, SCMA and RSMA, resource allocation and comparisons of access techniques. [Research results](publications.html).

## Intelligent surfaces

![Conceptual signal path via an RIS when the direct channel is blocked](assets/research/ris.svg)

Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, including RIS and STAR-RIS, offer control over radio wave propagation. This research includes channel models and the development of reflecting surfaces. [Related projects](projects.html).
