# About the laboratory

We study physical-layer technologies for 5G/6G communication systems: non-orthogonal multiple access, error-correcting coding, reconfigurable metasurfaces and multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) systems.

The laboratory is based at the Department of Telecommunications and Basic Principles of Radio Engineering, TUSUR, in Tomsk.
